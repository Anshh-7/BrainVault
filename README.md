BrainVault
Overview

BrainVault is a console-based quiz adventure game developed in C. The game challenges players through a sequence of memory, general knowledge, logic, and rapid-fire challenges.

Players progress through five vault stages while managing limited lives, earning points, maintaining accuracy, and achieving different ranks based on their final score.



How to Play:

Enter a Vault Explorer name.

Select Start Game from the main menu.

Complete the challenges in order.

Answer questions correctly to earn points.

Preserve your lives by avoiding incorrect answers.

Complete all five stages to unlock the final BrainVault.

View your final score, accuracy, and rank.

Check the High Scores section to compare saved scores.
